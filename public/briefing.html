Why

BD prep today takes three disconnected manual passes.

Before any client conversation, an associate has to build a picture of the prospect: what is happening to the company, who sits around it in its value chain, and which experts in the network map to each development. Today that means three separate efforts done by hand. Scan recent news and filter the PR noise. Sketch the supply chain from memory and scattered sources. Then search Mosaic by guesswork, one keyword combination at a time. The cost is not just time - roughly [X hours] per prospect. The first pass rarely gets beyond the obvious players, so second-order expert angles (a chipmaker's equipment supplier, that supplier's biggest customer) never make it into the search. And because every associate assembles the picture differently, prep quality varies with whoever happens to be doing it.

What

Value Chain Explorer collapses those three passes into one search.

  1. A mapped value chain - upstream suppliers, downstream customers, and corporate/strategy layer, drawn from recent news and public filings, going one layer deeper than the obvious names.
  2. Impact-ranked signals - every development scored by how hard it hits the company (revenue, cost, or risk), pinned to the exact part of the chain it affects, with a plain-language "why it matters" line an associate can repeat straight into a client conversation.
  3. Ready-to-paste Mosaic searches - for each signal, the kind of expert worth calling, with company, title, industry, function, and region filters already written out.

What it deliberately does not do

The tool touches no internal GLG data, no expert records, and no client content. It never names a real person from the network - it describes a representative profile and drafts the search. The associate runs the actual Mosaic search and makes the call. Public sources in, search strategy out.

How

Four steps, one pass.

01
Search
Reads recent news and filings on the anchor company, filtering routine PR for developments that actually move the business.
02
Map
Places each finding on the chain: suppliers upstream, customers downstream, and leadership, capital, and strategy in the corporate layer.
03
Score
Ranks every signal by estimated impact on the anchor - revenue, cost, or risk - highest first.
04
Match
Drafts the expert archetype and the Mosaic filters for each signal. Paste them into Mosaic to find the real person.
