What it is

Value Chain Explorer is an internal research tool built for BD prep. An associate types an anchor company — a prospect, a client, anything relevant to a mandate — and the tool reads recent news and public sources, works out who that company buys from, sells to, and depends on internally, and lays it out as a map. Every news signal it finds is scored by how much it actually matters to that company and pinned to the exact part of the chain it affects.

The output is not just a map. Each signal arrives with the GLG experts already matched to it, and their Mosaic search filters pre-written — company, title, industry, function, region — so an associate can go from "what's happening with this company" to a shortlist of experts to source in one motion.

How it works
01
Search
Reads recent news and filings on the company, filtering out routine PR for developments that actually move the business.
02
Map
Places each finding on the chain: who supplies the company, who buys from it, and what's moving inside its own leadership and strategy.
03
Score
Ranks every signal by how hard it actually hits the company — revenue, cost, or risk — highest impact first.
04
Match
Attaches the GLG experts suited to each signal, with Mosaic search terms already drafted and ready to paste.
Why it matters for CS associates

Any node becomes its own starting point
